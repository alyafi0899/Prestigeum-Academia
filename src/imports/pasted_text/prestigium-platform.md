Create a complete modern responsive web platform for **Prestigium Academia**, an engineering education and professional training platform.

The website must combine:

1. Public marketing website
2. Engineering training/event discovery
3. User authentication
4. Training registration
5. Participant dashboard
6. Event attendance with digital signature
7. Automatic certificate access
8. Articles/news
9. Gallery
10. About Prestigium Academia
11. Admin/event management interface

The design must feel **premium, professional, technical, modern, trustworthy, and educational**, targeted at engineering students, fresh graduates, junior engineers, and professional engineers.

# 30. DESIGN PRINCIPLES

The final design should feel like a combination of:

Premium engineering academy
Professional corporate training platform
Modern education technology
Industry-focused learning ecosystem

Avoid:

* Generic template appearance
* Excessive gradients
* Excessive glassmorphism
* Cartoonish UI
* Overly colorful elements
* Cluttered dashboards
* Tiny typography

Prioritize:

* Strong visual hierarchy
* Excellent spacing
* High-quality engineering photography
* Clear CTAs
* Professional typography
* Accessible forms
* Easy event discovery
* Frictionless registration
* Clear participant status
* Strong trust and credibility

Use the Prestigium Academia brand palette consistently while allowing white/neutral backgrounds for readability.
